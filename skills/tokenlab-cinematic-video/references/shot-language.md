# Shot language

Plain camera words that video models understand. Use one item from each group that matters for the shot. Google's Veo prompt guide lists the same kinds of terms: camera position and motion, composition, focus and lens, and ambiance.

## Shot size (composition)

| Term | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Extreme wide shot | The place; people are small |
| Wide shot | The whole subject and its surroundings |
| Medium shot | The subject from the waist up |
| Close-up | The face or one object fills the frame |
| Extreme close-up | One detail: an eye, a label, a drop |
| Two-shot | Two people in one frame |
| Over-the-shoulder | One person seen past another's shoulder |

## Camera angle

| Term | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Eye level | Neutral, natural |
| Low angle | The subject looks big or strong |
| High angle | The subject looks small or alone |
| Top-down / bird's-eye | Pattern and layout |
| Worm's-eye | Extreme low view upward |
| Aerial / drone | Large spaces, establishing shots |

## Camera move (one per shot)

| Term | Motion |
| --- | --- |
| Static / locked-off | No camera motion |
| Pan left / right | The camera turns side to side |
| Tilt up / down | The camera turns up or down |
| Dolly in / out | The camera moves toward or away from the subject |
| Tracking / follow | The camera moves with the subject |
| Orbit / arc | The camera circles the subject |
| Crane up / down | The camera rises or drops |
| Handheld | Small natural shake, documentary feel |
| Slow push-in | Gentle dolly-in that builds focus |

Speed words change the feel: "slow" reads calm and premium, "fast" reads urgent.

## Lens and focus

| Term | Look |
| --- | --- |
| Wide-angle lens | Deep space, stretched edges |
| 35mm | Natural view with context |
| 50mm | Close to human vision |
| 85mm | Flattering portraits, soft background |
| Macro lens | Tiny details, very shallow focus |
| Shallow focus | Subject sharp, background soft |
| Deep focus | Everything sharp |
| Soft focus | Dreamy, low contrast |
| Rack focus | Focus moves from one subject to another |

## Light and ambiance

| Term | Mood |
| --- | --- |
| Golden hour | Warm, low sun, long shadows |
| Blue hour | Cool, calm, after sunset |
| Overcast | Soft, even, no hard shadows |
| Hard key light | Strong shadows, drama |
| Backlight / rim light | Glowing edges, separation |
| Practical light | Light from lamps or screens in the scene |
| Neon night | Saturated color spill, wet reflections |
| Warm tones / cool tones | Overall color temperature |

## Style

Name a medium or genre, not a living artist: "film noir", "sci-fi", "documentary", "1970s film grain", "stop-motion", "cel-shaded animation", "paper cut-out animation".

## Sound (models with audio)

- Ambience: "distant traffic", "wind in tall grass", "quiet room tone".
- Effects: "boots on wet stone", "a door creaks open".
- Dialogue: put the exact words in quotes.
- Silence: say "no music" if music would cover the scene.
